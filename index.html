How it works

Put a job on the board. We’ve got it.

Every client gets a shared monday.com board. It’s where jobs come in, where reviews happen and where nothing gets lost in an inbox.

  1. 1Add a job

    A brief, any files and the deadline. Tight timelines welcome.

  2. 2We get notified

    Reviewed first thing and assigned to the right senior creative.

  3. 3“We’re on it”

    You see who has it and when to expect a first look.

  4. 4Review cycle

    Proofs and revisions stay with the job. Motion is reviewed frame by frame on Frame.io.

  5. 5Delivered on time

    Final files, ready to use, with source files on request.

  6. 6Repeat

    The next job lands and we already know your brand. Each one moves faster than the last.

Good fit

Are we a good fit?

We work best as an ongoing partner. Most of our clients are marketing teams, and we also work alongside creative directors and their in-house design teams. They need 40 to 200 hours of design and motion every month, and a team they can count on to deliver it. Most of all, we’re here to be helpful.

A great fit
  • Marketing teams with a steady flow of work, month after month.
  • Launches, campaigns, events and sales support running at the same time.
  • Tight timelines and a brand that has to look right every time.
  • Teams that want the same people on their brand every week.
  • Creative directors and in-house design teams who brief the work and need more hands. We work as an extension of your team, in your files and your process.
Usually not a fit
  • One-off projects with no ongoing work behind them.
  • We make an exception when a first project is the start of a longer partnership.
