01  How it works

Put a job on the board. We’ve got it.

Every client gets a shared monday.com board, free to join. It’s where jobs come in, where reviews happen and where nothing gets lost in an inbox.

Already have your own board, Figma libraries and review process? We work in yours. Tight deadline? We plan for it, and when something truly can’t wait, we make room.

05  Good fit

Are we a good fit?

We work best as an ongoing partner, and we keep our client list small on purpose: a handful of teams we can do our best work for, every day.

Most are B2B marketing teams in software, security and financial services; some are creative directors whose in-house teams need more hands. Typically they need 40 to 160 hours of design and motion a month, with room to flex from 20 to 200.

Why a studio like ours

vs. Freelancer

Instead of a freelancer

A full senior team across design, motion, UX, print and apparel, with someone ready for every job.

vs. New hire

Instead of a new hire

No ramp-up. We start on the first job and flex with your month, with no payroll when it slows.

vs. Big agency

Instead of a big agency

Direct access. No account managers or junior pool, and even our creative director designs, with 30 years in the business.

With your team

Alongside your team

More hands, same brand. We step into your process, take the overflow and the deadline crunch, and help the brand look its best along the way.

How we work together

A job or two at our hourly rate, with an estimate before we start. We start from your guidelines and past work, and, where you want it, help sharpen them as we go. Most retainer clients began this way.

The details

A great fit

  • Marketing teams with a steady flow of work, month after month.
  • Launches, campaigns, events and sales support running at the same time.
  • Busy calendars and a brand that has to look right every time.
  • Teams that want the same people on their brand every week.
  • Creative directors and in-house design teams who need more hands, working in your files and your process.

Usually not a fit

  • One-off projects with no ongoing work behind them. A first job or two to try us out is always welcome.
