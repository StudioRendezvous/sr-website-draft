Good fit

Are we a good fit?

We work best as an ongoing partner, and we keep our client list small on purpose: a handful of teams we can do our best work for, every day. When you need a lot done fast, several of us take it on at once, across projects or a whole campaign. Most are B2B marketing teams in software, security and financial services; some are creative directors whose in-house teams need more hands. Typically they need 40 to 160 hours of design and motion a month, with room to flex from 20 to 200.

Right in the range most of our clients work in, month after month.

Why a studio like ours

Instead of a freelancer

A full senior team across design, motion, UX, print and apparel, with someone ready for every job.

Instead of a new hire

No ramp-up. We start on the first job and flex with your month, with no payroll when it slows.

Instead of a big agency

Direct access. No account managers or junior pool, and even our creative director designs, with 30 years in the business.

Alongside your team

More hands, same brand. We step into your process, take the overflow and the deadline crunch, and help the brand look its best along the way.

How we work together

Try us first

A job or two at our hourly rate, with an estimate before we start. We start from your guidelines and past work, and, where you want it, help sharpen them as we go. Most retainer clients began this way.

Put a first job on the board

Annual retainer

A team you can count on all year. Your year is already on the calendar, so we commit to it: a set block of hours each month at our best rate, invoiced at the start of the month. Unused hours roll over and stay yours until they’re used. Hours and rates are reviewed each year.

Hourly

Room to flex when needs swing month to month. Invoiced at month end for the hours used.

Before you sign

Senior team, need to know

Every job, released or not, is done by our senior team, never outside freelancers. Launches and unreleased products are seen only by the people on your jobs. Send us your vendor security review and we’ll complete it before the first job.

Compliance review

Legal and compliance rounds are expected and built into the schedule from day one.

Busy months

When your month spikes, the whole team picks it up. Share your launch calendar and we plan around it.

How it works

Project delivery steps

  1. 01

    Add a job

    A brief, any files and the deadline.

  2. 02

    “We’re on it”

    A senior creative who knows your brand picks it up, and you see when to expect a first look.

    Final files, ready to use, and source files on request.

    Repeat

    The next job lands on a team that already knows your brand.
