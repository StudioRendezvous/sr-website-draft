How we work together

Put a job on the board. We’ve got it.

Every client gets a shared monday.com board, free to join. It’s where jobs come in, where reviews happen and where nothing gets lost in an inbox. Already have your own board, Figma libraries and review process? We work in yours. Tight deadline? We plan for it, and when something truly can’t wait, we make room.

  1. 1Add a job

    A brief, any files and the deadline.

  2. 2“We’re on it”

    A senior creative who knows your brand picks it up, and you see when to expect a first look.

  3. 3Best first draft, fast

    Refined by a senior designer before it reaches you. Our creative director leads, or we work to yours.

  4. 4Review

    Proofs and revisions stay with the job. Motion is reviewed frame by frame on Frame.io.

  5. 5Delivered

    Final files, ready to use, and source files on request.

  6. 6Repeat

    The next job lands on a team that already knows your brand.
